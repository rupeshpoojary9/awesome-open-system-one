# Contributing

Thanks for helping keep this list high-signal and honest.

## Scope

This list is about the **open** System One ecosystem. An entry belongs if it is:

- an **open-source or runnable** System One model / reproduction, or
- an **independent evaluation or benchmark** of typed decision models, or
- **calibration / selective-prediction** tooling (temperature scaling, conformal methods, abstention), or
- **constrained / structured decoding** tooling that makes output schema-valid by construction.

Out of scope: closed or waitlisted products themselves (link the official docs once, under Reference), and projects that merely *use* the closed Jev, those belong on the other awesome-jev lists.

## Quality bar

- Open source or openly readable. No paywalls, no signup walls.
- Real and maintained, or clearly notable.
- Accurately described: the one-line summary must match what the project actually does. No marketing claims restated as fact, attribute vendor numbers as claimed.

## Format

```
- [Name](https://link) - One factual sentence, under ~25 words.
```

Put the item in the most specific section, use the canonical link, and **verify it works** before submitting.

## Submitting

Fork, add your entry on its own branch, and open a PR explaining what you added and why it meets the scope and bar. No self-promotion of unmaintained or off-topic projects.
